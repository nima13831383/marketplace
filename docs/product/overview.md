# Product Overview

## Status

The complete product brief has not yet been supplied. The project is a **Marketplace** with an approved canonical catalog, Store Offer, commercial CTA, and future Seller monetization model. Product category, audience, geography, detailed transaction behavior, and final business policies remain open.

## Purpose

To be completed from an approved product brief. This section should explain the user problem, the value the Marketplace provides, and the intended outcome.

## Scope

### Confirmed

- The project concerns a Marketplace application.
- Requirements will be provided incrementally and must remain internally consistent.
- The application will contain four surfaces in one Laravel application: Admin Panel, Seller Panel, Customer Panel, and API.
- Admin, Seller, and Customer are approved primary account classifications, not a complete role or permission catalog.
- A unified User identity and multi-Store Seller participation are approved conceptual boundaries.
- The Marketplace maintains Master Products and Store-specific Offers; search/archive presentation is Master Product-first.
- Sellers submit Offers for Marketplace/Admin moderation and Master Product matching.
- Commercial CTAs may be tracked and classified for future billing through a central Billing Mode.
- Articles, comments on Articles/Master Products, and Ticketing are approved high-level capabilities; their workflows remain open.

### Not yet defined

- Marketplace type and inventory or service category.
- Detailed target-user outcomes and geographic or regulatory scope.
- Supply and demand model.
- Customer Panel feature catalog and discovery, transaction, fulfillment, support, and settlement behavior.
- Master Product/Offer fields, pricing, stock, search ranking, billing plans, fraud policy, integrations, article taxonomy, comment moderation, and ticket workflow.
- Success measures and launch scope.

## Non-goals for the current phase

- Building the frontend or backend.
- Implementing or installing the approved portal technologies.
- Selecting unapproved production technologies without requirements. Laravel and the documented architecture/authentication capabilities are approved foundations; remaining product and operational choices stay open.
- Requiring a separate frontend application; the current direction is server-rendered/server-driven Laravel portals plus an API in the same application.
- Designing a production database schema.
- Defining executable API or business logic.
